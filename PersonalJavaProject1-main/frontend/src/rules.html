Rules

Player(s) must first chose their desired shape to play and press to play. The player with the "X" shape will go first and chose any cell they would like to play in. Once player "X" has picked a cell, the miniboard of that cell will be played and followed by player "O"'s choice. The whole board inside that cell is played until a player wins, marked by their shape over the board, or a tie displayed by a minus symbol over the board.

The cycle repeats until a player scores 3 in a row or if the game ends in a tie.
